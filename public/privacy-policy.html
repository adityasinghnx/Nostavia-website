LEGAL & PRIVACY COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 2026  |  Nostavia Health Inc.

🛡️ Our Core Privacy Promise: Nostavia Health engineered SOMA Edge Privacy to strip personally identifiable information (PII) on-device before any health data touches our cloud reasoning servers. You own your health data, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Overview

Nostavia Health ("Nostavia", "we", "us", or "our") provides intelligent health analytics, longevity telemetry, and clinician-supervised decision support software. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, protect, and handle your data across our mobile applications, web portals, and connected services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our platform, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Identification & Account Data

  • Account Details: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and account credentials.
  • Profile Information: Preferred language, contact preferences, and health goals.

B. Health, Biometric & Telemetry Data

  • Connected Device Metrics: Sleep data, continuous heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen, physical activity, and wearable metrics.
  • Clinical & Biomarker Inputs: Self-reported health history, lab biomarker values, medication logs, and clinical briefs uploaded with explicit user consent.

C. Technical & Usage Information

  • Device Information: Operating system, hardware version, unique device identifiers, and network connection details.
  • Application Logs: Diagnostic telemetry, error reports, and feature interaction metrics.

3. SOMA Edge Privacy & Data Minimisation

Health data requires architectural protection. Nostavia is built on a proprietary SOMA Dual-Brain architecture:

  • On-Device De-Identification: Your personal identifying details (name, email, SSN/national ID, contact info) are isolated and stripped locally on your client device using the SOMA edge layer.
  • De-Identified Cloud Reasoning: Only anonymised, mathematical representations of clinical data are transmitted to our cloud engine for risk assessment and predictive modeling.
  • Purpose Limitation: We only process data strictly necessary to deliver health insights and service functionality.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Providing personalized longevity recommendations, metabolic insights, and clinical briefs.
  • Facilitating secure data sharing with your authorized healthcare provider or clinic.
  • Maintaining app stability, debugging, and protecting system security.
  • Fulfilling legal, regulatory, and healthcare compliance obligations.

5. Global Regulatory Compliance

Nostavia Health is built to comply with international health data regulations:

  • India DPDP Act (2023): Explicit consent mechanisms, purpose-bound processing, and data principal deletion rights.
  • UAE Health Data Residency (Law 17/2026): In-region data processing options and localized privacy safeguards.
  • US HIPAA & EU GDPR Aligned: End-to-end AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and stringent access controls.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal health data to third-party advertisers or data brokers. We only share data under these conditions:

  • With your Authorized Clinicians: When you explicitly choose to share health briefs with your treating clinic or physician.
  • Service Providers: Standard cloud infrastructure providers (e.g. encrypted database hosting) bound by strict non-disclosure and data processing agreements (DPAs).
  • Legal Mandates: When required by valid law enforcement orders or judicial court subpoenas.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Deletion Options

You maintain full control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Export: Request a full copy of your health and profile data.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct or update inaccurate account information.
  • Right to Account & Data Deletion: You can delete your account and associated data at any time. Visit our Account Deletion Page or Data Deletion Request Page.

8. Data Security & Retention

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols including multi-factor authentication, cryptographic key management, and continuous security monitoring. We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable medical record retention laws.
