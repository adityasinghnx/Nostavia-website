Request Account Deletion

Use this form to submit an official request to permanently delete your Nostavia Health user account and associated personal data.

What happens when you request account deletion?

  • Permanent Data Erasure: Your user profile, credentials, connected health metrics, app telemetry, and personal preferences will be permanently purged from our primary servers.
  • Cancellation of Access: You will immediately lose access to your Nostavia Health mobile app account and active digital health briefs.
  • Turnaround Time: Account deletion requests are processed within 30 calendar days.
  • Legal & Regulatory Retention: Certain anonymised telemetry and financial transaction receipts may be retained for up to 7 years solely to satisfy statutory audit, tax, and healthcare regulatory requirements.
